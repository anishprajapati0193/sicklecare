FUTURE RESEARCH · EXPERIMENTAL

## Microneedle medicine patch

Microneedle patches use tiny needles that pass the outer layer of skin. Researchers are studying them as a way to give sickle cell medicines such as hydroxyurea and L-glutamine through the skin, in materials that release the medicine slowly, aiming for about a week from one patch.

### What researchers hope for

- Little or no pain when the patch is applied
- Fewer daily doses, which may help people keep to their treatment
- Skipping the gut and the liver's first pass, which may reduce side effects

### Limits in this project

- Experimental. Not an available treatment and not part of this demo
- The monitoring patch in this app only senses readings. It never delivers medicine
- Any link between readings and dosing would stay with the doctor. SickleCare AI never changes a dose
- Needs laboratory work, clinical trials and regulatory approval first

How AI is used and roadmap

## How AI is used

### AI can

- Read a prescription photo into a medicine list
- Summarize trends and symptom logs
- Say whether readings look like improving, stable or worse
- Draft a clinical summary for the doctor

### AI must not

- Diagnose sickle cell disease
- Predict every pain crisis
- Change doses or prescribe medicine
- Say normal readings rule out a crisis
